## Question 1: What does bloom mean in simple english?

## Answer
In the context of this application, **a "bloom" is just another name for a "post" or a "message."**

Think of it like this:
*   On **Twitter (X)**, you write a **"Tweet."**
*   On **Facebook**, you write a **"Post."**
*   In **PurpleForest**, you write a **"Bloom."**

### Why is it called that?
The app is named **PurpleForest**. In a forest, flowers "bloom" when they open up and show themselves to the world. So, in this app, when you share a thought or a message, you are "blooming."

### How the words are used:
1.  **As a Noun:** "I just read a very funny **bloom**." (I just read a funny post).
2.  **As a Verb:** "I am going to **bloom** about my lunch." (I am going to post about my lunch).
3.  **The New Feature (Re-bloom):** This is exactly like a **"Retweet"** or a **"Share."** You are taking someone else's bloom and showing it to your own followers.

### Logic Check:
Now that you know a "bloom" is just a post, let's look at the logic of **Re-blooming**:

If you "Re-bloom" my post:
*   **You** are the "Re-bloomer."
*   **I** am the "Original Author."
*   The **"Bloom"** is the content we are both sharing.

**In your database, do you think we should save the actual text of the message twice, or should we just save a "link" that points to the original message?**
